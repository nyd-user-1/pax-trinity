PAX

A guided tour

No account neededBest on a laptopAbout 15 minutes
1

The front door

  1. Open the address. The plate of boxes is the contents: one box for each part of the platform.
  2. Click any box to open it, then scroll to How to use it and Still to build.
  3. Step through the boxes with the arrows at the dialog's top right.
2

Audience

  1. Pick a topic under Index on the left, such as Politics & government. Each row is one survey question, its latest headline after the dash.
  2. Click a question. Its data opens beside the list.
  3. Switch to Trend to see every wave it was asked.
  4. Press the star before the question's title. It joins Favorites at the top of the left column.
  5. Press the globe at the end of Data · Crosstab · Trend for the question's one-page dossier: the headline, the trend by wave, the crosstab and related questions.
3

Clips

  1. Open Clips. The first clip plays.
  2. Open the three dots on the Partners block and choose a desk: Voter Freedom, Unite America, Yes on 3 or PAX.
  3. Click a title in the list to watch that cut.
4

Reports

  1. Open Reports and choose Massachusetts Message Testing.
  2. Read the executive summary: each video's lift in ballot support against its own wave's placebo.
  3. Scroll to a wave's videos. Each card gives the video's lift in ballot choice and in comprehension; Watch video plays it.
  4. Scroll to Subgroup results. Each bar is the share saying Yes in one subgroup, marked where the video differed from the placebo.

Detectable means the video met the 90% statistical threshold against the placebo. Inconclusive means no difference was detected, which is not proof of no effect.

5

Lift benchmarks

  1. Open Lift on its Archive: ad-level effects from campaigns' own randomized tests.
  2. Pick a metric card, such as Lift: candidate favorability. The treatments rank by their lift; a bar fades inside its own margin.
  3. Click a lift for its margin, respondents and study.
