AAssistantChat flow 2: Write and record a video

Subject: Write and record a video in Chat

Write and record a video in Chat

Hi RECIPIENT_NAME,

The road from an idea to a video on file runs through one chat:

1. Press the camera beside the box. I ask a few short questions, one at a time: who you are, your town, and why open primaries matter to you.
2. I draft a script in your words: a hook under 12 words, one concrete number, about 25 seconds spoken, ending with what the viewer should do. Each line that leans on evidence carries its source.

Read Lift in a minute

Lift sets every tested video's effect side by side, so a strong result stands out from an ordinary one before you scale it.

What one lift means

Hi RECIPIENT_NAME,

AAssistantSurvey flow 3: Two rules for consensus

Subject: Most agree, or every group agrees?

Most agree, or every group agrees?

Hi RECIPIENT_NAME,

“Most people agree” and “everyone agrees” aren't the same thing, and the results carry a switch so you can read the same votes both ways.

- Trinity: most of the room is on one side, and no opinion group has a majority on the other.
- Walkthrough: every opinion group agrees at 7 in 10 or more. The least agreeable group decides, so a big group can't carry a small one.

The Walkthrough rule is stricter and slower to find anything; with few votes or a divided room it can come back empty. The switch is remembered in your browser.

Compare the rules
