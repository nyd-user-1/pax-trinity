Partner topline

The /r/ readout given a public face a partner can put its name on. Figures: the Open primaries Consensus survey as seeded (its votes are invented). Marks: public/partners; Unite America's is a placeholder until its real mark is on file.

  1. One page per survey at a public address (/r/<slug> today is the reader's own results; this is its face for anyone).
  2. The partner sets two colours and a mark; everything else is the survey's own.
  3. What it leaves out on purpose: opinion groups' make-up, raw counts per statement, anything about the respondents.
  4. The address in the citation (pax.nysgpt.com) is a stand-in; the public one isn't chosen.
