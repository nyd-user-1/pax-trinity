Definitions
Support — the share choosing Yes after watching, compared with the share after the placebo.
Comprehension — the share correctly identifying what the measure would change, compared with placebo.
Lift — the video's percentage minus the placebo's, in percentage points. Every comparison is against the same test's placebo, never another video.
Detectable — meets the test's 90% statistical criterion. Inconclusive — not detected; not proof of zero effect.
Weighted n — respondents weighted to population benchmarks; not an effective sample size.
Flagged — kept in the record, kept out of the score: an arm too small to judge, or no placebo to compare against.
