Resonance v1

AssessesHow much a video moved people, measured in a randomized test: the change in stated support and in comprehension versus the same test's placebo.
Higher meansLarger movement against placebo, on more fronts, across more of the audience. 0–100.
ReferenceThe video's own placebo arm — never other videos. The scale's anchors are stated below, so a score means the same thing in a later test.
Is notA forecast for an unpublished video, a measure of lasting opinion change, or proof of what caused the result.
